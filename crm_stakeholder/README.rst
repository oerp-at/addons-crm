This module adds stakeholders to leads and opportunities: partners with
a role (from **project_stakeholder**) and a note. The partner form shows
in which leads/opportunities the partner is a stakeholder.

**Table of contents**

.. contents::
   :local:

Usage
=====

- Define the roles in *CRM > Configuration > Stakeholder Roles* (shared
  with project stakeholders).
- Add stakeholders in the *Stakeholders* tab of a lead/opportunity.
- The tab *Stakeholder in Lead/Opportunity* on the partner lists them
  per partner.
